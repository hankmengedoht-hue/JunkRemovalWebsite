FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your Christmas tree service.

How does delivery & setup work?

Choose your tree and book a delivery window. We pick it up, bring it inside, place it in the stand, secure and level it, water it, and vacuum up any loose needles before we leave.

Can I choose my own tree?

Yes — select by height, or let us know if you have a specific tree in mind. Just tell us your preference when you book.

Do I need my own tree stand?

If you already have one, we'll use it. If not, we can provide a stand for an additional charge — just let us know when you book.

Will you water the tree and clean up?

Always. Every delivery includes watering the stand and vacuuming any needles or debris from the setup before we leave your home.

What's included in end-of-season removal?

We pump out the water, remove the tree from the stand, carefully carry it out of your home, vacuum any fallen needles, and take the tree away for disposal or recycling.

Do I need to be home for delivery or removal?

In most cases, yes — we need someone present to confirm placement. If you have a unique situation, give us a call and we'll work something out.

Can I bundle delivery and removal?

Yes — our Complete Christmas Service bundles delivery, setup, and end-of-season removal together, and it costs less than booking each service separately.

What areas do you serve?

Charleston and the surrounding Lowcountry — the Peninsula, James Island, Johns Island, Mount Pleasant, Folly Beach, North Charleston, West Ashley, Daniel Island, Sullivan's Island, Isle of Palms, Kiawah, and Hanahan. Not sure? Just call.
