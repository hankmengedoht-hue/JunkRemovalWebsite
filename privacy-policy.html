Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us.

Last Updated: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Palmetto Christmas Concierge ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information submitted through this website.

Information We Collect

When you use the booking form, contact form, or review form on our website, we may collect the following information:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Your service address, if you're requesting a delivery or removal
  • Any additional details you voluntarily include, such as preferred dates, tree preferences, or a message

We only request information that is reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, schedule your service, or publish a review you've chosen to submit.

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our forms is used solely for legitimate business and communication purposes, including:

  • Responding to your questions or inquiries
  • Scheduling and confirming a Christmas tree delivery, setup, or removal you've requested
  • Contacting you regarding a request you submitted
  • Publishing a review on our website, if you submit one through our review form (using the name and neighborhood you provide)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Form Processing

Our website uses Formspree to process and deliver form submissions. When you submit information through one of our forms, the information you provide may be transmitted to and processed by Formspree in order to deliver it to us.

Formspree's handling of information is subject to its own privacy practices and policies.

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information submitted through our website from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain information submitted through our forms only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, complete your service, maintain our business communications, or fulfill legitimate business or legal obligations.

Cookies and Tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising tracking technologies. We do not track your browsing activity or build advertising profiles.

The only information we collect is what you voluntarily submit through our booking, contact, or review forms. If that changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe those practices.

Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services necessary to operate the website or process information submitted through it, such as Formspree and our website host. These third parties may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

We do not control the privacy practices of third-party services and encourage you to review their policies when appropriate.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we maintain about you.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the information below.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, business practices, or applicable legal requirements.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
