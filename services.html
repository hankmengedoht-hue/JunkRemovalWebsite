Simple Process

How It Works

From choosing your tree to the day it comes back out, here's the whole process.

1

Choose Your Tree

Select a tree by height, or let us know if you have a specific tree in mind. Book online, call, or text us.

2

We Pick It Up

We collect your tree and bring it directly to your home on the delivery window you booked.

3

We Set It Up

We bring it inside, place it in the stand, secure and level it, and fill it with water.

4

We Clean Up

We vacuum any loose needles from the installation and leave your space ready for decorating.

5

Enjoy Christmas

You decorate and enjoy your tree. We've already handled the hard part.

6

We'll Take It Away

After the season, book removal and we'll pump the water, remove the tree, carry it out, and clean up.

Christmas Tree Delivery & Setup

Skip the tree lot, the roof rack, and the needles in your car. Choose your tree and we bring it straight to your home, place it exactly where you want, secure it in the stand, level it, fill it with water, and vacuum up before we leave. We top off the water once at setup; refilling it during the season is up to you.

✓ Tree pickup & delivery   ✓ Indoor placement & setup   ✓ Leveling & initial watering   ✓ Needle cleanup

End-of-Season Tree Removal

When Christmas is over, we take care of the mess. We pump the water out of the stand, remove the tree, carefully carry it out of your home, vacuum any fallen needles, and take the tree away. Every tree is composted locally here in Charleston, so nothing ends up in a landfill.

✓ Water & stand removal   ✓ Careful carry-out   ✓ Needle cleanup   ✓ Local composting

📞 Call Now
