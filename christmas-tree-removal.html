Two Ways to Get Rid of Your Tree

Christmas Tree Removal & Pickup, Done for You

In-Home Christmas Tree Removal

The full-service option. We come to your home, pump the water out of the stand, take the tree out, carefully carry it through your house, vacuum the fallen needles, and haul it away. You don't lift a finger, and your floors stay clean.
