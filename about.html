How We Started

Our Story

Christmas should feel magical. That's the idea behind Palmetto Christmas Concierge, started by Hank and Jacob to make one of the best parts of the season easier: getting a real tree through the front door, set up right, and taken back out in January without the mess.

We noticed that having a real Christmas tree usually means a trip to a crowded lot, a struggle to get it home in one piece, and a fight with the stand once it's inside. We knew Charleston homeowners deserved a better way: a service that handles the heavy lifting so the family can just enjoy the season.

So we built this service the same way we'd want it done for our own family: show up when we say we will, treat every home with care, and leave things cleaner than we found them.
